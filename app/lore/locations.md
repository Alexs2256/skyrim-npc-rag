# Political Geography: The Nine Holds

## Whiterun Hold
Whiterun is the economic heart of Skyrim, driven by its centralized geographic location and fertile farmland. Its initial neutrality makes it the most critical strategic flashpoint of the Civil War. The capital city is fortified by watchtowers, a single drawbridge, and defensive gates. It is historically built around the Skyforge—an ancient forge predating the city—and is divided into three distinct vertical tiers:
The Plains District: The lower tier, hosting the bustling marketplace, local taverns, and commercial shops. The Wind District: The middle tier, serving as a quiet, traditional residential area dominated by the companion mead hall, Jorrvaskr. The Cloud District: The highest peak, completely dominated by the palace of Dragonsreach. Notable Residents: Jarl: Balgruuf the Greater, Steward: Proventus Avenicci, Jarl's Housecarl: Irileth, Thane's Housecarl: Lydia.

## Windhelm
Windhelm is one of the oldest cities in Tamriel and the oldest city in Skyrim. Constructed from massive, ancient grey stone, it serves as a fortress-like port city along a major northern river. Windhelm serves as the capital of the Stormcloak Rebellion. The city suffers from severe racial tensions, manifested in the segregation of the Dunmer (Dark Elf) refugees into a slum known as the Gray Quarter. Many traditionalist Nords view the non-Nord population with immense suspicion. The city center is dominated by the Palace of the Kings. Notable Residents:
- Jarl: Ulfric Stormcloak
- Housecarl / Second-in-Command: Galmar Stone-Fist
- Steward: Jorleif
- Court Wizard: Wuunferth the Unliving
- Dissident Figure: Brunwulf Free-Winter (advocate for non-Nord citizens)
- Prominent Families: The Shatter-Shields and the Cruel-Seas.

## Markarth
Markarth is an impenetrable mountain city built directly into a steep stone canyon in western Skyrim. Unlike Nord cities, Markarth is constructed entirely out of ancient Dwemer (Deep Elf) stone architecture, built by an extinct race long before Nords settled the area. Its economy is entirely dominated by the Silver-Blood family, who control the nearby Cidhna Mine. The region faces ongoing guerrilla warfare from the Forsworn, native Reachmen fighting to reclaim their stolen homeland. Notable Residents: Jarl: Igmund, Steward: Thongvor Silver-Blood, Court Wizard: Calcelmo (Renowned Dwemer Scholar), Scholar's Assistant: Aicantar, Investigator: Eltrys

## Solitude
Solitude is the official capital of Skyrim and the stronghold of Imperial power within the province. Built atop a colossal natural stone arch overlooking a major northern trading port, Solitude controls Skyrim's shipping and maritime commerce. It houses the Blue Palace, the headquarters of the Imperial Legion, and the historic Bards College. Notable Residents: Jarl / High Queen Candidate: Elisif the Fair

## Riften
Riften is a lakeside port town in southeastern Skyrim, heavily reliant on fishing and mead production. Riften is defined by systemic corruption. The wealthy Black-Briar family completely controls the local government and guards. The city acts as the official headquarters for the Thieves Guild, which operates openly out of the subterranean sewers beneath the city, known as the Ratway. Notable Residents: Jarl: Laila Law-Giver, Steward: Anuriel

## Winterhold Hold
Once a massive and powerful historic capital, Winterhold was largely destroyed by the "Great Collapse"—a catastrophic disaster where most of the city sheared off a cliffside into the sea. The remaining village is a frozen, hollowed-out settlement. Perched on a nearby isolated spire is the College of Winterhold, an independent magical academy that survived the collapse entirely unscathed. This has caused the superstitious local Nords to deeply resent and blame the mages for the town's ruin. It is an intensely cold region, and most of the hold is covered in snowy tundra plains and high mountain ranges. Along the coast is a polar environment of glaciers and icebergs. The surrounding holds are Eastmarch, to the south; and The Pale, to the west.

## Falkreath Hold
Falkreath is a somber, heavily forested southern town located near the border of Cyrodiil. It is uniquely defined by its massive, sprawling graveyard, which houses thousands of soldiers from centuries of historic battles.

## Morthal
Morthal is a small city in Skyrim located in the dense Drajkmyr Marsh of Hjaalmarch. Its ruling Jarl is Idgrod Ravencrone. Morthal is isolated, fog-shrouded settlement built at the edge of a treacherous northern marsh. Its populace is highly superstitious, isolated, and frequently plagued by local ghost stories and rumors of monsters in the wetlands. Notable Residents: Jarl: Idgrod Ravencrone (Known for her prophetic visions) Steward: Aslfur

## Dawnstar
Ruled by Jarl Skald the Elder, Dawnstar serves as the capital of the Pale and sits along Skyrim's northern coast. The town's economy centers on its mines and its harbor, and it doubles as a garrison outpost watching over the northern shoreline. Along with Winterhold and Windhelm, Dawnstar is one of the coldest settlements in the province, blanketed year-round in snow and ice. Fort Dunstad lies to the southeast, not far from the Hall of the Vigilant near the Winterhold border. Notable Residents: Jarl: Skald the Elder
